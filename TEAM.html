NOGOGENI
 ITS TEAM

The Nogogeni ITS Team, founded in 2011 at Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) in Surabaya and named after a legendary fire-breathing dragon in Javanese mythology, is a student-led automotive research team dedicated to designing and building energy-efficient vehicles. Beginning with internal combustion and ethanol-powered prototypes, the team expanded between 2014 and 2017 into the Urban Concept category and battery-electric propulsion, designing practical city-style eco-cars.

From 2018 to 2021, Nogogeni achieved consistent podium finishes at the national Kontes Mobil Hemat Energi (KMHE) and represented Indonesia at the Shell Eco-marathon Asia, before advancing from 2022 onward into next-generation lightweight engineering featuring carbon-fiber monocoque chassis, aerodynamic bodies, custom drivetrains, and real-time telemetry systems to maximize energy efficiency per kilometer.
